About this site, and how it decides

CompareAI exists because most “best AI tools” pages are ranked by commission rate and written by someone who has never opened the product. This one is desk-researched, sourced and explicit about what it does not know.

How the rankings are made

Each category page compares five or six tools on four things: the price you actually pay, how well the tool does its stated job, how much friction there is in using it, and whether the free tier is honest. The order is set by those factors. Commission is not one of them.

Where the data comes from

  • Pricing is read from the vendor's own pricing page, not from a reseller blog. Both monthly and annual figures are recorded, because vendors increasingly quote the annual number as the headline.
  • Features come from vendor documentation and pricing tables. Where a capability is unclear or plan-dependent, the page says so instead of assuming.
  • Affiliate terms come from the program's own page or its network listing. Where only third-party aggregators publish a number, the term is labelled “verify” rather than stated as fact.

What this site does not do

  • It does not run a lab or a benchmark suite. These are desk-researched buying guides that prioritise accurate pricing and fit over synthetic scores.
  • It does not publish invented “success rates”, fake user counts, or made-up test hours.
  • It does not accept paid placement. There is no sponsored tier and no way to buy a ranking.
  • It does not claim a tool is best because it pays the most. Three category winners pay nothing at all.

How often it is checked

Prices and program terms were verified in November 2026. AI pricing changes constantly — several tools on this site repriced during 2026, and two affiliate programs closed. Every comparison page carries a “last checked” date. If a vendor page disagrees with this site, the vendor page is correct.

Corrections are welcome and get fixed. If a price or program term is wrong, send the vendor URL and the page it appears on. Corrections are logged below rather than quietly edited.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and buy a subscription, we may receive a commission from the vendor. The price you pay is the same either way.

Here is the part most sites leave out:

  • Affiliate programs are free to join. We never pay for placement, and we are not paid unless you subscribe.
  • Commission does not influence ranking, scoring or verdicts. Where a tool with no program is the better choice, it is ranked higher anyway — see GitHub Copilot, Cursor and Midjourney.
  • Only programs we could confirm are labelled “open”. Programs that closed are labelled closed. Where terms are unclear, they are labelled “verify”.
  • We do not claim to have tested a product hands-on unless the page explicitly says so. These are documented comparisons, not laboratory reviews.

The disclosure also appears at the top of every comparison page, before the first affiliate link, as the FTC's guidance requires. If you are building a site from this template, keep it there — moving it to the footer is both bad practice and, in the US, potentially unlawful.
