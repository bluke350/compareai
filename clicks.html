Counts are aggregate only: no cookies, no IP addresses, no user identifiers. Each click is also tagged with what the button pointed at — only a configured affiliate tracking link counts as earnable; a vendor fallback, or an affiliate application page with no tracking, does not. Earnable clicks is therefore the number that can actually produce commission; the gap against Total clicks is traffic you sent for free.
