Ten dollars a month separates the entry plans, which makes the decision look trivial. It is not. The real difference is how much keyword data that money buys, whether AI-visibility tracking is included or sold as an extra, and how much of the client report you still assemble by hand.

Verdict

The recommendation

SE Ranking is the better buy for most agencies; Semrush is the better buy for the invoice

If your week is finding keyword gaps, briefing writers and checking whether the page moved, SE Ranking gives you more data for less money and includes the AI-visibility view. If your week ends with a document a client reads, Semrush's coverage and exports save hours that are worth more than the $10 difference. Do not buy both: at this overlap, one of them will sit unused.

FAQ

Three questions agencies ask

Is SE Ranking a genuine Semrush alternative?

For rank tracking, site audits and AI visibility, yes — and it costs less per tracked keyword. Semrush still wins on backlink depth, competitive research and client-ready reporting. If the month's work is finding gaps and tracking results, SE Ranking covers it. If the month's work is proving results to a client, Semrush is the safer invoice.

Should I pay for both tools?

Only when a client pays for the overlap. At $129 and $139 a month they duplicate most day-to-day tracking. The common split is one platform for research and one cheaper tracker for client reporting. Pick one first and add the second when a retainer covers it.

Are the trials long enough to decide?

SE Ranking gives 14 days and Semrush gives 7. Test your own domain: import the keyword set you actually track and check whether the AI-visibility report covers the prompts your buyers type. Seven days is short for a team decision, so run Semrush's trial when the people who sign off are available.
