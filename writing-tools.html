In detail

The three that matter

Pros, cons and the verdict for the tools most readers actually shortlist.

Jasper — best overall

$69/mo monthly · $59/mo annual · 7-day trial

Winner

What works

  • Brand voice and knowledge assets keep output consistent across writers.
  • Campaigns and workflows handle multi-asset launches, not just single drafts.
  • GEO features help content get cited in AI answers, not only ranked.

What doesn't

  • $69 a month per seat is hard to justify for one blogger.
  • Business credit pricing is impossible to forecast before invoice time.
  • Seven days is a short trial for a team purchase decision.

Verdict: if content is a revenue channel and more than one person writes it, Jasper pays for itself in saved editing. If you write alone, buy Surfer plus a $9 writer and keep the change.

Visit Jasper

Surfer SEO — best value

$59/mo monthly · $49/mo annual · 7-day refund

Best value

What works

  • Briefs tell a writer exactly what to cover, in what order, at what depth.
  • The on-page score is a useful forcing function, not just a gimmick.
  • Cheapest way to add a real optimisation layer to your process.

What doesn't

  • It cannot write the article for you — that is the point, but people expect it to.
  • Following the score blindly produces stiff, repetitive copy.
  • Document caps rather than word caps make heavy months awkward.

Verdict: the highest-leverage $49 in content marketing. Pair it with Jasper, Writesonic or a human and stop guessing why a post didn't rank.

Visit Surfer

Writesonic — best for AI-search visibility

$99/mo monthly · $79/mo annual · no-card trial

SEO teams

What works

  • Tracks whether your brand shows up in ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overviews.
  • Content, audits and fixes live behind one login and one invoice.
  • The trial needs no card, so you can verify the data before paying.

What doesn't

  • It costs more than a dedicated writer with no permanent free tier.
  • The product does so much that the first week is genuinely confusing.
  • Article volume is capped per plan, and extra articles cost more.

Verdict: buy Writesonic for the citation data, not the prose. If you already pay Semrush, check its AI visibility module first — you may not need another subscription.

FAQ

Writing tools, answered

Is Jasper worth it over ChatGPT?

If you need brand-consistent output from several writers and a workflow to manage campaigns, yes. If you write alone and edit carefully, ChatGPT at $20 or Claude at $20 will get you most of the way for a third of the price. Jasper's advantage is governance, not raw model quality.

Do AI writing tools hurt SEO?

Only when they produce generic, unedited pages at volume. Google's guidance targets low-value scaled content, not AI text as such. The tools that help are the ones forcing research and structure — Surfer's briefs, Writesonic's audits — rather than the ones generating 50 posts from one keyword.

Which of these pays the most as an affiliate?

Surfer at 75–125% of the first month on a $99+ plan is the highest one-time payout. Jasper at 25–30% recurring over 12 months wins on lifetime value for a $59/mo customer. Grammarly has the lowest payout but the highest conversion rate, so it often earns more per click.

Can I promote these without a big audience?

Yes. None of the programs on this page require a minimum audience except Semrush (1,000 monthly visitors), which isn't in this category. Approval is usually same-day for Writesonic and Rytr, and a few days for Jasper. Some programs do ask where you'll publish, so have a real page live first.

Are these prices monthly or annual?

Both are shown. The bold figure is monthly billing; the smaller one is per month when you pay annually. Jasper's $59 and Writesonic's $79 are annual prices quoted per month — the monthly prices are $69 and $99.
