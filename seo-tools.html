Winner

What works

  • AI Visibility Toolkit puts prompt tracking beside keyword data.

  • Reporting is client-ready without extra work.

What doesn't

  • $139 a month before you add the AI toolkit.

Verdict: buy it if you bill clients for SEO reporting. If you only need rank tracking, SE Ranking does most of the job for less, and Frase plus Otterly covers content and citations for under $80.

FAQ

AI SEO tools, answered

What is an AI SEO tool, exactly?

Two different jobs get the label. The first is using AI to do classic SEO — briefs, drafts, audits — which Frase and Surfer cover. The second is optimising to be cited by AI answers (GEO/AEO), which Semrush, SE Ranking, Ahrefs Brand Radar and Otterly track. Most teams now need a bit of both.

Do I still need Google rankings if AI answers the question?

Yes. AI Overviews and most assistants ground their answers in pages that already rank or are well cited. Losing organic visibility usually means losing AI visibility too — which is why the tools that combine both are worth the premium.

Which of these pays the most as an affiliate?

Semrush pays the largest single payout — roughly $100 to $450 per sale plus $10 per trial, with a 120-day cookie. SE Ranking pays 30% on every rebill with the same 120-day cookie, which often beats a one-off bounty for a $129/mo customer. Frase pays 30% for 12 months, rising to 35% and 40% at volume.

Why include Ahrefs and Otterly if they pay nothing?

Because a comparison that only lists paying tools is an advert. Ahrefs is the data benchmark and Otterly is the cheapest AI-visibility entry point; leaving them out would make the page less useful and easier to distrust.

Is it too late to start an SEO affiliate site in 2026?

No, but the easy wins are gone. Comparison and “is X worth it” pages still convert because buyers are close to a decision. The pages that fail are generic “best tools” lists with no testing, no prices and no opinion.
