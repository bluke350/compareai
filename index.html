Method

How these rankings are made

Questions

Quick answers

Do I need to pay anything to use the affiliate links on this site?

No. Joining an affiliate program is free — every one listed here costs $0 to apply to, with no membership or listing fee. You only ever pay for a product if you decide to buy it, and affiliate links never change the price.

How current is the pricing?

Pricing and program terms were checked against vendor pages in November 2026. AI tools reprice often, so every page carries a “last checked” date and points to the vendor page for the final number.

Which category is easiest to build an audience around?

Voice and video convert best because the output is visible and the plans are recurring. Writing is the most crowded. Coding has the highest intent but almost no cash affiliate programs — the two that pay are Tabnine and CodeRabbit.
