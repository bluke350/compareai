ElevenLabs — best overall

$6/mo Starter · $22/mo Creator · free 10k credits

Winner

What works

  • Prosody and emotion are a clear step above the rest.
  • Instant voice cloning on the cheapest paid plan.
  • Dubbing and 90+ languages in the same product.

What doesn't

  • Credits vanish quickly on long-form narration.
  • Free tier cannot be used commercially.
  • Cloning consent checks slow down some workflows.

Verdict: start here. At $6 to test commercially, there is no reason to guess. It is also the easiest affiliate sell on this site: 22% for a full year, 90-day cookie, and people keep subscribing.

Murf AI — best for e-learning

$29/mo Creator · $19 annual · 2 hours of voice

E-learning

What works

  • Canva and PowerPoint plugins put narration where the slides are.
  • Per-hour pricing is easier to budget than credits.
  • 24-month affiliate window doubles the payout ceiling.

What doesn't

  • Expression lags ElevenLabs on emotional scripts.
  • Cloning is an enterprise add-on, not self-serve.
  • Free tier is a one-time 10 minutes, not monthly.

Verdict: if your deliverable is a narrated deck or module, the workflow beats the voice quality. If your deliverable is a podcast, it doesn't.

Visit Murf

Cartesia — best for real-time agents

$5/mo entry · free credits · API-first

Agents

What works

  • Latency low enough for natural conversation.
  • Cheap to prototype and scale.
  • Built for streaming rather than file export.

What doesn't

  • Limited voice library compared with consumer tools.
  • No timeline or studio UI.
  • No affiliate program, so no revenue angle.

Verdict: the right tool for a voice agent and the wrong one for a YouTube channel. Judge it on response time, not on how pretty a demo sounds.

FAQ

Voice tools, answered

Can I monetise YouTube videos made with ElevenLabs?

Yes, but only on a paid plan. The free tier is non-commercial. Starter at $6 includes a commercial licence, which is why it is the plan most creators should test with.

Is voice cloning legal?

Cloning your own voice is fine. Cloning someone else requires their explicit written consent, and several jurisdictions now require disclosure when synthetic voice is used in advertising or political content. Both ElevenLabs and Murf enforce consent verification — do not try to work around it.

ElevenLabs or Murf for a podcast?

ElevenLabs. Its prosody handles conversational pacing better, and the dubbing studio is useful for clip distribution. Murf is the better choice when the script is tied to slides.

Which voice program pays the most?

Murf nominally: 20% for 24 months versus ElevenLabs' 22% for 12. On a $19/mo Murf plan that is about $91 per customer over two years; on a $22/mo ElevenLabs plan it is about $58 over one year. But ElevenLabs converts far more easily, so revenue per click usually favours it.

Do I need to pay for an API separately?

No. All four include API access in their subscription tiers, though rate limits and commercial terms vary. Cartesia and PlayHT are built API-first; ElevenLabs sells consumer and API usage on the same credit system.
